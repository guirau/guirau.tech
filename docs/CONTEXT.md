## What this is

A single-page scroll portfolio site at **guirau.tech**. Its job is to **close
new clients in the tech space** — it is a client-acquisition funnel, not a
personal homepage or an art project.

## Who it's for (owner)

**Alejandro Guirau** — freelance AI/ML engineer and consultant, operating as
an independent contractor (Freiberufler, registered in Germany), working fully
remotely from Koh Phangan, Thailand.

- ~7 years across software, data, ML/AI engineering, and DevOps.
- Background at HelloFresh (Berlin), Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), and
  Telefónica. (An Upwork catalog also lists Santander — confirm before it
  ships publicly; other materials do not cite it.)
- B.Sc. in Physics. Also a former documentary filmmaker and TV presenter
  (hosted a science/climate series on Spanish national TV) and a lifelong
  educator. These are *seasoning*, not the site's headline — engineering
  leads.
- Signs "Ale Guirau" casually, "Alejandro Guirau" formally.

## What he sells

Productized AI services, structured as a price ladder from a low-cost entry
audit up to a flagship end-to-end AI app build. Full offer details, prices,
and tiers live in **CONTENT.md** (single source of truth). Every offer starts
with a free discovery call as the CTA.

## Core differentiator

**Production-grade engineering** — real trained ML and deployed systems, not
prototypes or no-code patchwork. This is the through-line for the whole site:
proof of real production work is what separates him from the crowd of AI
generalists.

## The funnel (site strategy)

**Demos build trust → productized offers convert → free discovery call closes.**
Scrolling should feel like a story that earns the conversion at the end, not a
stack of disconnected sections. Trust is established first via live, interactive
ML demos (a CLIP-based image classifier and a SegFormer + SAM2 segmentation
demo) that prove real capability; the offers then convert that trust; the
discovery call closes.

## Audience

Broad/generic positioning for now: businesses of any size or industry needing
production AI apps, agents, and automations. The intent is to build the funnel
generically first, then re-skin it to a specific niche later (candidate niches
explored elsewhere: creator economy, performance marketing). The build must
keep audience-specific language in **swappable blocks** so niching later does
not require restructuring.

## Success looks like

Qualified prospects in the tech space reaching the end of the page and booking
a free discovery call. Secondary: the site reads as credible and tasteful
enough that agencies and larger clients feel comfortable referring or hiring.
